---
title: MicroSD brands with reported compatibility issues
---

Some genuine MicroSD cards have been reported to cause compatibility problems with Nintendo DS flashcarts, DSi and 3DS consoles. These reports have been collected from the DSi & 3DS community. This has largely been observed on genuine but budget brands.

Games & Homebrew may fail to load at random or have strange glitches, and flashcart menus or custom DSi menus such as TWiLight Menu++ may fail to load or only load intermittently. This can happen even if the MicroSD card appears to work fine on a computer.

The exact cause remains unknown. We suspect these budget brand cards are tuned either to get as much speed as possible out of their budget components or to prioritise sustained transfers for streaming. Either of these could cause compatibility problems on older, slower devices.

Reported brands include:

- Intenso
- Biwin
- Hama
- Kootion
- Philips
- Emtec
- MediaRange

Not every card from these brands is necessarily affected. However, **we recommend avoiding them when purchasing MicroSD cards for these devices** due to the reported issues.

If you are unsure what brand to go for, [check out the list here](microsd-fakes#where-to-buy-genuine-microsd-cards) as they will generally work fine on these devices.
